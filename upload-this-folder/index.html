Sale Rules

Staff sign-ins

Everyone signs in with their own email and password. Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed) and are never shown. Staff choose their own password the first time they sign in.

Delivery Zones

Cloud database

Copies every sale, payment, delivery, stock change and setting to MFD's own cloud database. The till keeps working without internet and catches up automatically when the connection is back.

Backup & Restore

Data is saved on this device (and in the cloud database when it is connected). Download a backup regularly — especially before clearing the browser cache or switching computers — so nothing is ever lost.

Admin Action Log

Every refund, deletion and backup restore is logged automatically with who did it and when.
